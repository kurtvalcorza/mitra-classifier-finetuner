# Finetuner deps. The [mitra] extra pulls Mitra's runtime dependencies (omegaconf,
# huggingface_hub, and the Mitra model code); plain autogluon.tabular does NOT include them,
# and Mitra fails to import at fit time.
# torch/CUDA are pinned by the base image tag (pytorch:2.8.0-cuda12.8), which satisfies

# PyPI-default wheel. Do NOT re-pin torch here: a bare "torch==2.8" lets pip replace the cu128
# build with a cu-default one and break sm_120. The Dockerfile asserts the locked torch/CUDA.
# scikit-learn (stratified split/cap) and huggingface_hub (weights resolution) are provided,
# version-pinned, by autogluon.tabular[mitra]==1.5.0 — do not re-pin them here and risk a clash.
